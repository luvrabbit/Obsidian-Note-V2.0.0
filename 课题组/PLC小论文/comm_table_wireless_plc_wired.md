## Notes To Support Introduction Narrative (Optional)

- Koshkouei 2024 (JoES 79) Table 1 is a good reviewer-facing anchor: it explicitly positions PLC as low complexity/cost because it reuses the bus-bar, versus wireless (antenna integration/multipath/shielding) and wire harness (weight/cost/vibration robustness).
- Vincent 2021 reports CAN literature bit error rates on the order of `3.1 x 10^-9` in a "normal" environment (their ref. [60]), while their PLC errors in the combined CAN+PLC logging experiment were dominated by collisions/processing and dropped to zero in shorter runs when CAN transmission was removed.

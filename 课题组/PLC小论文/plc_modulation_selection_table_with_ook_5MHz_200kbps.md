# PLC Modulation Selection Comparison (With OOK@5 MHz / 200 kbps Rationale)

This is a variant of `plc_modulation_selection_table.md` with an added “relevance to this work” column to directly justify selecting **OOK at 5 MHz / 200 kbps** using the extracted paper evidence.

| Modulation | Spectral Efficiency (Qualitative) | Hardware Complexity / Size | Cost (Qualitative) | Power (Paper-Reported Examples) | Robustness (Paper-Reported / Qualitative) | Suitability For Battery Modules (Evidence) | Reported Battery-PLC Examples (Frequency / Data Rate / Error) | Relevance To This Work (Chosen: OOK @ 5 MHz / 200 kbps) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| OOK (2-level ASK) | Low | Lowest (envelope / comparator demod possible; simple digital drive) | Lowest | `<100 mW` normal; `~48 mW` without LED; `~150 mW` peak per board (Vincent 2021) | More sensitive to amplitude attenuation/noise than FSK/PSK; mitigated by conservative bit timing and operating in a favorable frequency band. | Strong fit when payloads are short and node count is high (cell-level smart sensing): lowest BOM/power/area and proven in battery stacks. | Vincent 2021: TI `THVD8000DDFR`, `5 MHz` carrier, OOK; `<0.005%` message error rate; modem supports up to `500 kbit/s` (RS-485, `10 cycles/bit` rec.) but implementation limited to `115.2 kbps` (RS-232). Landinger 2020: OOK at `20.40 MHz`; used `500 kbit/s` verification; `1000` packets, CRC-derived BER `0`; attenuation `17 dB` downlink / `7 dB` uplink. | **Selected** because our application is short telemetry where per-node simplicity/power/cost dominate over spectral efficiency. `5 MHz` is supported as a robust, low-noise choice in battery PLC: it avoids low-kHz impedance attenuation and avoids common switching-noise bands (`100s kHz–3 MHz`) (Vincent 2021), and `5–10 MHz` is identified as an optimal/low-attenuation sub-range in a 21700 channel+BER study (Hao 2025). `200 kbps` at `5 MHz` implies `~25 carrier cycles/bit` (5e6/200e3), providing extra margin vs the `10 cycles/bit` robustness guidance used for `500 kbps` at `5 MHz` on THVD8000-class modems (Vincent 2021). |
| FSK | Low to Medium | Low to Medium (freq generation + discriminator/PLL) | Low to Medium | Nature 2025 reports chip power `~21.50–21.69 mW` (overall chip, includes sensing + comms) | More tolerant to amplitude variation; Nature 2025 reports error rate `~1e-5` while recovering `<1 mV` amplitude (with on-chip signal correction / FEC context). | Good fit for ultra-low data rate but very high reliability at extremely small signal amplitude (implantable internal sensing). | Nature 2025: FSK; selected `1,200 bps` baud; amplitude `<1 mV` recovered with error rate `~10^-5`. | Not selected for baseline link because the demonstrated robust regime is at very low baud (`1200 bps`) and targets extremely small modulation amplitudes; our link target is `200 kbps` for routine monitoring packets. Consider FSK if future requirements shift to “very weak amplitude, very high reliability, very low rate” internal sensing. |
| QPSK / PSK (incl. modem QPSK) | Medium | Medium (coherent demod; tighter analog/DSP than OOK/FSK) | Medium | (Not a single board-power number reported in Hao 2025; performance-driven evaluation) | Hao 2025 reports BER `~10^-6` over `5–30 MHz` with frequency-dependent degradation; warns `12.5–15 MHz` due group delay/BER. | Strong candidate when reliability is paramount and modem complexity is acceptable; provides frequency-selection guidance for packs. | Hao 2025: Yamar `SIG102` EVB, QPSK, adjustable `5–30 MHz`, `115.2 kbps`; BER `1.0×10^-6` to `1.17×10^-6` (no error correction); recommends `5–10 MHz` and `20–30 MHz`; not recommended `12.5–15 MHz`. | Not selected because coherent/modem-based PSK typically increases per-node cost/complexity relative to OOK. It does, however, independently support that **low MHz (5–10 MHz)** is a favorable band, which we use to justify selecting `5 MHz` carrier even with OOK. |
| QAM (high-order) | High (in theory) | Highest (linearity/EVM requirements; DSP; channel adaptation) | Highest | Often instrumentation/modem dependent in battery studies | Strongly frequency- and order-dependent in battery channel; can degrade severely at some frequencies for high orders. | Suitable only if higher throughput is required and complexity/adaptation is acceptable; overkill for small telemetry payloads. | Koshkouei 2022: QAM tested `10–200 MHz` (symbol rate `100 kHz`); `1024-QAM` BER up to `0.48` at some frequencies; recommends `40–80 MHz` and `105–148 MHz` for minimum BER/SER across tests. | Not selected because our throughput target is already met at `200 kbps`, while QAM would raise modem complexity and require carrier/order adaptation to avoid frequency-dependent failures observed in battery channels. |

# Jordan标准型矩阵幂级数定理
$f(z)=\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}a_{i}z^{i}$，收敛半径为$R$
$$
J=\begin{bmatrix}
\lambda & 1 & 0 & \dots & 0 \\
0 & \lambda & 1 & \dots & 0 \\
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots \\
0 & \dots & \dots & \lambda & 1 \\
0 & \dots & \dots & \dots & \lambda
\end{bmatrix}_{n×n}
$$
若$\lambda < R$，则$f(J)=\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}a_{i}J^{i}$收敛，且
$$
f(J)=\begin{bmatrix}
f(\lambda) & f'(\lambda) & \frac{f''(\lambda)}{2!} & \dots & \frac{f^{n-1}(\lambda)}{(n-1)!} \\
0 & f(\lambda) & f'(\lambda) & \dots & \frac{f^{n-2}(\lambda)}{(n-2)!} \\
0 & 0 & f(\lambda) & \dots & \frac{f^{n-3}(\lambda)}{(n-3)!} \\
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots \\
0 & 0 & 0 & \dots & f(\lambda)
\end{bmatrix}
$$

# 矩阵幂级数定理
$f(z)=\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}a_{i}z^{i}$，收敛半径为$R$
$A=P^{-1}JP$，其中$J$为$A$的Jordan标准型
$$
J=\begin{bmatrix}
J_{1} & 0 & \dots & 0 \\
0 & J_{2} & \dots & 0 \\
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots \\
0 & 0 & 0 & J_{S}
\end{bmatrix}
$$
若$A$的谱半径$\rho(A)=\max(\lambda _{i})$，$\lambda$为$A$的特征值

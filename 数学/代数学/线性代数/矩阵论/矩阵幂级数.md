$$
J=\begin{bmatrix}
\lambda & 1 & 0 & \dots & 0 \\
0 & \lambda & 1 & \dots & 0 \\
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots \\
0 & \dots & \dots & \lambda & 1 \\
0 & \dots & \dots & \dots & \lambda
\end{bmatrix}_{n×n}
$$

$$

f(\lambda) & f'(\lambda) & \frac{f''(\lambda)}{2!} & \dots & \frac{f^{n-1}(\lambda)}{}
\end{bmatrix}
